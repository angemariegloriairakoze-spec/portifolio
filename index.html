About Me

Who I am & what I care about

AG

I'm Irakoze Ange Marie Gloria, a student developer with a passion for building things that live on the web.

From frontend interfaces to backend APIs and databases, I love turning ideas into real, working projects. I focus on clean code, thoughtful UX, and learning by doing—whether it's a real-time chat app, a full-stack dashboard, or experimenting with new tools.
